Memno is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to act as a personal link and note vault. It goes beyond simple bookmarking by automatically fetching rich metadata for every saved link, ensuring useful context is always available at a glance.<br><br>

<b>Core Features</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Rich Previews:</b> Automatically saves links with their title, description, and image previews.</li>
<li><b>Personal Notes:</b> Add notes to every saved item for reminders, thoughts, or annotations.</li>
<li><b>Instant Metadata:</b> Quickly fetches site information from almost any URL.</li>
<li><b>Quick Access:</b> Generates unique 6-digit short codes for easy retrieval of saved entries.</li>
<li><b>Offline-First:</b> Keeps the knowledge base accessible even without an internet connection.</li>
<li><b>Modern Interface:</b> Includes dark mode support and a clean minimalist interface built with Flutter.</li>
</ul>

<b>Versatile Use Cases</b><br>

Memno can be used for organizing research, archiving useful content, or maintaining a personal knowledge collection.

<ul>
<li><b>Knowledge Base:</b> Store study materials, research links, and academic references alongside notes.</li>
<li><b>Read Later Collection:</b> Save tutorials and articles with personal annotations for future reading.</li>
<li><b>Product and Recipe Vault:</b> Organize shopping links, recipes, and custom notes in one place.</li>
<li><b>Web Archiving:</b> Curate interesting discoveries from blogs, Reddit, and social platforms with personal commentary.</li>
</ul>

<b>Technical Foundation</b><br>

Memno is built using Flutter and Dart, with Hive used as the local database for fast and reliable offline storage.
