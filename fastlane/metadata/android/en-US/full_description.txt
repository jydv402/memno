Memno is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to act as a personal link and note vault. It goes beyond simple bookmarking by automatically fetching rich metadata for every saved link, ensuring useful context is always available at a glance.

Core Features

• Rich Previews: Automatically saves links with their title, description, and image previews.

• Personal Notes: Add notes to every saved item for reminders, thoughts, or annotations.

• Instant Metadata: Quickly fetches site information from almost any URL.

• Quick Access: Generates unique 6-digit short codes for easy retrieval of saved entries.

• Offline-First: Keeps the knowledge base accessible even without an internet connection.

• Modern Interface: Includes dark mode support and a clean minimalist interface built with Flutter.

Versatile Use Cases

Memno can be used for organizing research, archiving useful content, or maintaining a personal knowledge collection.

• Knowledge Base: Store study materials, research links, and academic references alongside notes.

• Read Later Collection: Save tutorials and articles with personal annotations for future reading.

• Product and Recipe Vault: Organize shopping links, recipes, and custom notes in one place.

• Web Archiving: Curate interesting discoveries from blogs, Reddit, and social platforms with personal commentary.

Technical Foundation

Memno is built using Flutter and Dart, with Hive used as the local database for fast and reliable offline storage.
